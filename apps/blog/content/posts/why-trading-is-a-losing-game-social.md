## LinkedIn Post

**I lost $3,000 trading crypto. Here's what the research says about why.**

Before building One Day Investor, I was a sporadic crypto trader. Not a daily one — which is part of the problem. Once a month, I'd see Bitcoin pumping, open Binance in a rush, buy near the top, and watch the position bleed out over weeks.

The total damage over two years: more than $3,000.

I thought I was unusual. I wasn't. Academic research across 12 major studies paints a consistent picture:

- 97% of persistent day traders lose money (Brazilian study, 19,646 traders)
- 73-81% of retail crypto investors lost money (BIS, 95 countries)
- 93% of professional fund managers underperform the S&P 500 over 20 years

The irony? If I had simply bought and held the same crypto I was trading, I would have made money. Bitcoin has outperformed almost every asset class over any 4+ year period.

The asset was fine. My behavior was the problem.

This experience is why I built One Day Investor around a monthly ritual instead of daily trading. Track your savings rate, your salary growth, your net worth. Check your portfolio once a month. The other thirty days, live your life.
